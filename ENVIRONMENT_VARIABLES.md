# Environment Variables

I valori reali non vengono mai committati.

## SUPABASE
`SUPABASE_URL`, `SUPABASE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY`, `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` (server-only).

## OPENAI
`OPENAI_API_KEY` server-only, `OPENAI_MODEL_SIMPLE`, `OPENAI_MODEL_MEDIUM`, `OPENAI_MODEL_COMPLEX`, `OPENAI_IMAGE_MODEL`.

## META
`META_APP_ID`, `META_APP_SECRET`, `META_REDIRECT_URI`, eventuali version/scopes centralizzati.

## LINKEDIN
`LINKEDIN_CLIENT_ID`, `LINKEDIN_CLIENT_SECRET`, `LINKEDIN_REDIRECT_URI`, versione API configurabile.

## GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE
`GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID`, `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET`, `GOOGLE_GBP_REDIRECT_URI`.

## TELEGRAM
`TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN`, `TELEGRAM_WEBHOOK_SECRET`.

## STRIPE
`STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`, `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` solo quando billing viene attivato.

## APP
`APP_ENV`, `APP_URL`, `SOCIAL_PUBLISHING_ENABLED`, `ENCRYPTION_KEY_CURRENT`, `ENCRYPTION_KEY_VERSION`.

Vedi `.env.example` per i placeholder.
